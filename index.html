Privacy Policy

Peel the Pile · Neonwhitepink · Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Peel the Pile (the "Game") and the third-party services built into it collect, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Game on both iOS (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play). The Game is developed and published by Neonwhitepink ("we", "us").

  • The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location permission.
  • Your game progress, coins, unlocked robots and levels, and sound settings are stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob and measures how it is played through GameAnalytics. These services collect device identifiers and usage data.
  • On iOS, the Game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used. If you decline, the IDFA is not accessed.
  • Optional in-app purchases are paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your card or billing details.
  • You can limit tracking and ad personalization at any time in your iOS or Android settings.

1. Information stored on your device

Your level progress, in-game coins, unlocked robots and levels, the items you bought (such as ad removal) and your sound and music volume are saved locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

3. How we use information

We use the information above only to show ads that pay for the free Game, to deliver in-app purchases, to improve levels and difficulty, and to find and fix errors. We see analytics only as statistics in the GameAnalytics dashboard. We do not try to identify you personally, and we do not combine this data with other sources.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking on iOS. On iOS 14.5 and later, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether the Game may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is not accessed and ads are not personalized based on it. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad personalization on Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device). After that, ads are no longer based on your advertising ID.
  • In-app purchases are optional. The Game can be played in full without buying anything.
  • Stopping all collection. Uninstalling the Game stops all collection by the services in section 2.

5. Sharing of information

Information is shared only with the services listed in section 2, for the purposes described there. We do not sell your personal information for money. Under some US state laws, using advertising identifiers for personalized ads may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising"; you can opt out as described in section 4.

6. Data retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Apple or Google keeps a record of your purchases in your Apple ID or Google account, which lets you restore them. Google, GameAnalytics and Unity keep the data they collect for as long as their own policies state.

7. Security

The services above send data over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we use providers that apply industry-standard protections.

8. International transfers

Apple, Google, GameAnalytics and Unity may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under the safeguards described in their privacy policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, the quickest way to limit collection is the iOS or Android settings described in section 4. For any other request, write to us at the address below and we will help as far as we and our providers can identify your data.

10. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game and data was collected, contact us and we will work with our providers to delete it.

11. Links to other services

The Game has buttons that open store pages in the Apple App Store or Google Play: the Game's own page (to rate it), our developer page, and our other games shown in the menu. Apple and Google have their own privacy policies; this policy does not cover them.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or the services it uses change. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
